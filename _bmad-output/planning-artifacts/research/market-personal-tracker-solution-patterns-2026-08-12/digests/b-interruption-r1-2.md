## PATTERN VERDICTS

- **Intentional-delay wrapper before opening an app (one sec)** → **proven** → Peer-reviewed field data (PNAS 2023: 57% fewer opens; the continue/dismiss choice is the active ingredient) plus consistent 2026 reviews; works for mild habit scrolling, decays into tap-through within weeks, never blocks, and reviewers recommend escalating to hard lockout tools.
- **Self-set app timers enforced at the limit moment (Screen Time limits, Digital Wellbeing timers)** → **mixed** → They reliably create a real interruption and some users honor it, but the "One More Minute/Ignore Limit" and settings-override paths make them routinely bypassed (Apple forums, How-To Geek, 9to5Google); enforcement is only real with a passcode held by someone else or an external device.
- **Escalation tiers: warning → lock (Opal Deep Focus, Freedom Locked Mode, Forest Deep Focus)** → **proven** → The most reliable software pattern — multiple independent reviews and store reviews report these modes as "genuinely hard to bypass" and self-defeat loops (e.g., editing the limit at the 1-minute warning) drop away; still never unbypassable in pure software (Settings/VPN/disable-permission workarounds exist). The 2026 arXiv study confirms escalation and modality matter and interact with user impulsivity.
- **Gamified consequence (Forest's tree dies)** → **proven** → Strong, sustained user evidence (years-long use, 4.8★, ADHD testimonials, Mashable corroboration), but it is a self-initiated commitment device — it does not fire at the moment free time opens, so it solves "staying on task," not "what do I do right now."
- **Forced-physical-condition interruption (iOS Screen Distance)** → **proven as implementable, weak on behavior evidence** → The full-screen block that only clears when the phone is physically moved is a proven, shipped platform pattern (default-on for under-13s); evidence of durable behavior change is anecdotal (one strong personal testimony; ophthalmologists unsure of the health link).
- **Proactive free-time suggestion / well-being nags at the moment of distraction** → **unproven** → YouTube/TikTok take-a-break and bedtime reminders are dismissible one-tap prompts that experts call cosmetic; Google's Pause Point (10-sec pause + substitute activity suggestions + restart-required opt-out) is the strongest design yet but hasn't shipped and has no user evidence; academic boredom-detection prototypes (Borapp Buzz, Active-Hour) never reached mass adoption. Highest-risk, highest-upside area — adopt the *in-the-moment substitute-activity* mechanic from Pause Point/one sec, not the ambient-detection dream.

It's the platform-native validation of the whole "productive alternative at the moment free time opens" thesis.

- **Physical/commitment-device category** (Blok NFC card, timer lock boxes, kSafe-style devices): adjacent solution to the bypass problem; evidence so far is thin and largely vendor-marketing — deserves a dedicated round with review mining.
- **The arXiv gradual-escalation line of work** (Meinhardt et al. 2025, Rixen et al. 2023, Terzimehić) and Okeke et al. 2018 on relapse after screen-time reduction — useful for designing the warning→lock escalation UX with trait moderation.
- **r/nosurf and ADHD-community sentiment** on friction-apps vs hard blockers — Reddit returned blocked/security pages this run; retry with a different access path.

- Neutral, quantitative retention/engagement data for Opal, Freedom, or Forest (only App Store star counts and vendor claims).
- Direct Reddit community sentiment on bypassing self-set limits (network-blocked this run).
