## VERDICTS
- Q1 (per-weekday histogram / weekday-vs-weekend statistic in Streaks or Habitify) → **not-found** → Streaks' and Habitify's own docs/sites confirm only per-habit weekday masks (Streaks) and per-habit/per-timeframe stats (Habitify: trend, completion %, calendar, daily-average, total) — neither names any per-day-type or weekday-vs-weekend breakdown. That strengthens round-1, but we can only vouch for these two apps; the blanket "no shipped app does X" remains unverified.
- Q2 (Habitica weeklies / completion-based rescheduling) → **partially** → Fixed calendar-based "Every X Days" (2015) and calendar-based Monthly Dailies shipped; the #7824 requested behavior — rescheduling the due date off last completion — never shipped: maintainers said so in 2016, current wiki docs still say completion never changes the schedule, and a new request for it was still open/closed in July 2025.
- Q3 (manual backfill of logged study time + honesty sentiment) → **proven** → Backfill/edit-past-time is a shipped, documented feature in Study Bunny (week-view add/edit, official tutorial) and Toggl Track ("Manual Mode"); YPT not confirmed. Design/user sentiment is split and documented on both sides: anti-backfill apps argue it prevents gaming; pro-backfill apps argue forgetting-to-log backfill keeps the record honest and only faking days is cheating.
- Q4 (RoutineFlow per-day scheduling + free tier) → **mixed** → The r/AuDHDWomen claim is only half true: RoutineFlow has a weekly planner and routines can be assigned to days, but official docs never describe per-day-of-week routine templates, and users report per-day item sorting is limited/premium-gated. Free tier is confirmed as 1 routine (≤5 steps).
