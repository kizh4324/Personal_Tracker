# Digest — C (urgency & delivery) — r1

Accessed: 2026-08-12.

**Research summary:** For an ordinary third-party single-user productivity/habit app, both iOS and Android gate every *truly* interruptive channel behind approval or an app category the app doesn't qualify for. The patterns that are actually available (priority flags, Time Sensitive, IMPORTANCE_HIGH, widgets/badges) are proven mechanisms but each has a documented failure mode — top-tier inflation, per-app user revocation, or non-social-notification blindness — that the design must engineer around.

## PATTERN VERDICTS

- Todoist-style P1–P4 priority flags → **mixed** → Red color + top-sort proven, but documented "everything is P1" inflation neutralizes it; adopt only with a scarcity guardrail (e.g., one "Critical" slot per day).
- Things time-bucket urgency (Today/This Evening, red deadlines, no flag tiers) → **proven** → User reports (incl. GTD forum) show urgency conveyed by placement + red dates works and avoids flag inflation; strongest *in-app* visual-weight pattern.
- iOS Critical Alerts entitlement → **dead-end** → Apple-gated to health/safety; denials and silent multi-week reviews documented; users still must opt in per app.
- iOS Time Sensitive notifications → **proven (available)** → Only real urgency channel open to any app without approval; breaks Focus + Scheduled Summary, not mute; per-app off-switch makes overuse self-defeating.
- Android IMPORTANCE_HIGH (sound + heads-up) → **proven (mechanism)** → Available to any app, but user-downgradeable and capped below OS-reserved IMPORTANCE_MAX; no true "critical" tier for third parties.
- Android full-screen-intent alarm delivery → **dead-end** → Android 14+ auto-grant limited to alarm/call core apps; Play review rejections documented for a timer/productivity app.
- iOS Scheduled Summary / delayed delivery → **mixed** → User-chosen quiet tier; apps can't control membership, so it's a noise-reduction tool, the inverse of urgency.
- Persistent / resurfacing reminders ("rings until acknowledged", snooze loops) → **proven (as product pattern)** → Multiple shipped apps (Remylo, Remindly, Alarmate) plus Apple's own iOS 26 "urgent reminder → alarm" suggest it; but the "breaks through DND" variants require entitlements ordinary apps can't get — implement as in-app resurfacing + normal notifications, not OS-level persistence.
- Widgets/complications as non-clearing delivery → **proven (emerging)** → Survives the "clear everything" habit by not being a notification; evidence is product pages + reviews (esp. ADHD users), not controlled studies.
- Badge counts → **mixed** → Persistent and un-clearable, but disable-able, technically flaky (Background App Refresh; Apple Reminders iCloud bugs), and no evidence badges alone drive completion.
