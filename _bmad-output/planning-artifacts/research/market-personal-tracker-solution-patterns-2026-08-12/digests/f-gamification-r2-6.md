## VERDICTS
1. **Does the KDD 2020 paper report increased lesson completion, and for whom? What about message fatigue / bandit demotion?** → **proven** — Yes: +0.4% total lessons, +0.5% DAU, +2% new-user recurring retention over a strong baseline, via the Recovering Difference Softmax Algorithm; the recency penalty demoting recently-seen templates is core to the algorithm and explicitly attributed to novelty/fatigue theory. Caveat: the lift is from *better template selection inside* the character-voice pool, not from character voice itself.
2. **Does the UPenn/UCLA "goal slack" study exist and does it support streak-freeze forgiveness?** → **proven** — Sharif (Wharton/UPenn) & Shu (UCLA) published it in JMR 2017 (DOI 10.1509/jmr.15.0231) with a failure-persistence follow-up in OBHDP 2019. Field experiment: reserve goals → up to +20% steps, up to +40% more goal-days, and markedly better persistence the day after a miss. Duolingo's blog citation is accurate.
3. **Does a named-character voice measurably beat generic, and does it cause uninstalls/muting?** → **mixed** — The only quantified evidence is Duolingo's own, unpublished spokesperson claims (sad-Duo subject line 5–8% lift in lesson-taking; crying owl A/B-tested "to the teardrop" with a reported small uplift). No data tables, no independent replication, and zero published numbers on character-voice-driven uninstall or mute rates. Independent field data (Yonsei 2026) confirms emotional-anthropomorphic tone moves engagement but in a segment × tone × time-sensitive way, with effects decaying when notifications stop.
4. **Is one shared reward currency behaviorally superior to fragmented rewards?** → **not-found** — No study directly compares unified vs. multi-currency economies in habit/health apps. Nearest evidence is indirect: a JM 2024 field study found parallel game+value reward engines create "double postreward resetting" frictions (arguing *for* fewer parallel currencies), while a 2020 JMIR RCT found adding a premium currency to a quit-smoking app did *not* improve engagement or retention, and Dicheva et al. (2023) found a single virtual currency raised engagement but not intrinsic motivation or outcomes.

## LEADS
- Duolingo streak blog (Jan 2022) is itself a primary quantitative anchor: 2× Streak Freezes → +0.38% relative active learners/day, and 7-day streaks → 3.6× course-completion likelihood.
- "Push the Paw" field experiment (Yonsei; N=595 pet-app users): personalized notifications (owner+companion name) beat generic — relevant to companion-app notification design, but about personalization, not character voice.
- Quittr RCT (JMIR 2020, N=175): earned premium currency + mini-game produced no significant engagement/retention lift vs. control — a caution against reward-currency over-engineering.
- KDD 2020 dataset (Harvard Dataverse DOI 10.7910/DVN/23ZWVI) contains the ~200M-notification logs; could directly test fatigue/template-repetition patterns for a single-voice system.
- Japanese 2021 system paper (HIS): an anthropomorphic character delivering notifications improved motivation to reduce smartphone overuse (N=25) — niche support for persona-driven nudging.
