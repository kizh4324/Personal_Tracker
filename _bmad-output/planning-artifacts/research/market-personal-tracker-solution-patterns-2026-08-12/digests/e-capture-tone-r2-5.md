## VERDICTS
- **Silverman & Barasch (JCR 2023) → mixed** → Paper located and read in full: broken streaks durably-suppress-engagement is only partially proven (broken vs intact streaks change subsequent engagement in short-horizon experiments); "durably" was never longitudinally tested. The "worst for long streaks" half is NOT supported — the paper found no streak-length interaction (up to 20) and flagged longer streaks as an open question; the ">60 days abandonment" figure appears in no primary source reached.
- **U Penn/UCLA "goal slack" study → proven** → Sharif & Shu (OBHDP 163, 2021) is exactly the paper Duolingo's blog links (identical ScienceDirect PII); slack/emergency reserves beat rigid no-slack goals on persistence after failure (1 field + 4 lab). One design caveat: the reserve must be applicable immediately after the miss, not granted in advance.
- **Duolingo "Earn Back" as non-paid earned mechanic → mixed** → A free earned restoration exists but as a limited-time "Streak Revival" event (June 1–30, 2026), not a standing 2025 feature; the permanent recovery path (Streak Freeze/Repair) is gem-monetized. The 2025 UX Magazine claim is not corroborated as a permanent mechanic.
- **Todoist Ramble independent accuracy / sustainability → not-found** → No independent (non-vendor) accuracy benchmark exists — no error-rate or precision numbers anywhere. Best independent source is the Product Talk interview with Doist engineers, which reveals accuracy is deliberately traded for easy correction. No longitudinal evidence on whether voice capture sustains as a habit vs decays; the 40%→62% figure remains vendor-reported only.

## LEADS
- **Streaks field RCT:** NBER WP w34173, "Streak to Success? The Effects of Highlighting Streaks on Student Effort and Learning" (Aulagnon et al., Aug 2025) — Peru math-platform experiment, ~60,000 students; streak highlighting raised effort/achievement, strongest early in streaks. Corroborates S&B lab findings with real-field evidence; also documents Khan Academy retiring streaks over motivation concerns and Duolingo's own +1% DAU / +3% Day-14 retention A/B results (via Econsultancy 2023).
- **Follow-on streak work:** Silverman, Barasch & Small, "Hot streak!" (OBHDP 179, 2023); Mehr/Silverman/Sharif/Milkman, "The motivating power of streaks" (OBHDP 187, 2025) — newest streak research by the same team.
- **Slack-with-a-cost:** Sharif & Shu 2017, "The benefits of emergency reserves" (JCR) — slack with a price is preferred AND effective; UCLA Anderson Review write-up (Apr 2024) is a clean accessible summary.
- **Streak stakes are large:** Duolingo claims 15M+ users with 1+ year streaks and a ~13-year longest streak (duome.eu hall of fame) — massive latent value in restoration, consistent with "Streak Revival" and with S&B's finding that repair attenuates the broken-streak drop.
- **Ramble build detail:** Todoist help doc "Dictate to add tasks with Ramble" (2026-06-19) confirms real-time capture ("captures as you talk, not after"); max-productive.ai review states Gemini 2.5 Flash Live + 38 languages (affiliate, treat with caution). Worth benchmarking against Siri/Google voice capture ourselves — nobody has published that comparison.

## NOT FOUND
- No independent quantitative assessment of Ramble (or any 2025–26 in-app task-voice capture) — no accuracy/error-rate benchmarks, no Siri-vs-Google-vs-in-app head-to-head with numbers.
- No longitudinal/retention data on voice task capture (does it sustain as a habit or decay as novelty?) — nothing at all beyond NoteWordy's CHI 2023 note (<20% usage).
- The "abandonment risk highest >~60 days" statistic: no primary source; the one paper that could anchor it (S&B) instead found length-insensitivity and explicitly left long streaks untested.
- "Earn Back" as a permanent 2025 non-monetized Duolingo mechanic: no source found; verified reality is the June-2026 limited "Streak Revival" event plus gem-priced Streak Repair.
- The hypothesized "Cheng, Mukhopadhyay" slack paper: does not exist as cited; the actual UPenn/UCLA slack study is Sharif & Shu (2021).
