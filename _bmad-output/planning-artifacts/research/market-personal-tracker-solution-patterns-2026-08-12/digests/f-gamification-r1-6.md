## PATTERN VERDICTS

- **Companion/pet persona (Finch bird, Duo owl, Forest trees)** → **proven** → The only category with hard retention data: Finch posts D1/D7 of 54%/37% (~10M MAU) and near-perfect store ratings, attributed to the pet loop; Duolingo's mascot demonstrably drove engagement and culture. Caveat: novelty decay is real and documented (~4–6 weeks in peer-reviewed research; "outgrows the bird" reviews), so the companion works long-term only if content/progression keeps evolving.
- **Companion as the notification voice (Duo's nudges)** → **mixed** → Proven to move engagement (200M-notification bandit study, repeated press as "annoying but oh-so-effective"), but the same voice generates documented anxiety and complaints (Oulu survey quotes; Duolingo "regret nothing" standoff). Works as engagement; sentiment risk is real and should be tuned to be gentle rather than guilt-heavy.
- **Single shared reward currency across all activities** → **proven** → Finch's one stone-currency sink across every activity, Habitica's gold→custom-real-life-rewards bridge (with real user testimonials, incl. 1GP=$2 setups), and Forest's coins→real-tree redemption all exist and are central to each product's success. The "one game" framing (DoF's single-loop description) correlates with Finch's top-tier retention.
- **Non-punishing vs punishing mechanics** → **mixed** → Forgiveness is proven to help: Duolingo's Streak Freeze raised active learners, and the "slack" research supports it; hard punishment (Habitica HP-loss/death) produced documented frustration, escape-hatch features, and avoid-by-not-opening behavior. But loss aversion is NOT dead: streaks motivate (7-day streak → 3.6x completion) and Forest's mild "tree withers" punishment is cited as motivating. Verdict: absorb misses gently (no hard resets), but a light, recoverable stake (like Forest) is fine — avoid compounding multi-miss penalties.
- **Rewards scaling with stakes (urgent > routine)** → **unproven** → The mechanism exists (Habitica difficulty/value scaling, Forest session-length coin scaling, Finch Goal-of-the-Day bonus) but no retrieved evidence shows stake-weighted reward magnitude improves completion or retention. Adopt the mechanism; expect to validate it yourself.
